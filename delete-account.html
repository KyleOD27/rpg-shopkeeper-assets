Delete Account

The Adventurer’s Emporium — RPG Shop

You can request the deletion of your RPG Shop account and all associated data at any time. This applies to accounts used via the Android app The Adventurer’s Emporium, the website at rpg-shop.uk, the Discord bot, and the WhatsApp integration.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to rpgshopkeeper@gmail.com with the subject line: Account Deletion Request
  2. Include your RPG Shop username in the message body. If you linked a Discord or WhatsApp account, you may include that too to help us locate your record.
  3. We will confirm receipt and permanently delete your account within 30 days.
Email Us to Delete Your Account →

What Gets Deleted

The following data is permanently and irreversibly deleted upon request:

Data Outcome
Username and hashed password Permanently deleted
Email address (if provided) Permanently deleted
Linked Discord user ID Permanently deleted
Linked WhatsApp phone number Permanently deleted
Characters, party memberships, and party gold balance Permanently deleted
Item purchase and sale history Permanently deleted
Conversation and session logs Permanently deleted
Personal notes Permanently deleted
Submitted bug reports and feedback Permanently deleted

No personal data is retained after deletion. Aggregate, anonymised statistics (e.g. total number of transactions processed by the service) do not contain any personally identifiable information and are not affected.

Further Information

For more detail on how we handle your data, see our Privacy Policy. If you have any questions, contact us at rpgshopkeeper@gmail.com.
