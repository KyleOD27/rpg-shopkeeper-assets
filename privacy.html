Privacy Policy

RPG Shop — rpg-shop.uk

Effective date: 11 June 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how RPG Shop (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and stores information when you use our services — including the website at rpg-shop.uk, the Android app (The Adventurer’s Emporium), the Discord bot, and the WhatsApp integration.

1. Who We Are

RPG Shop is an independent project providing a free D&D 5e shopkeeper NPC for tabletop roleplaying groups. We are based in the United Kingdom and this policy is written in compliance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR).

For any privacy enquiries, contact us at: rpgshopkeeper@gmail.com

2. What We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the service.

Account information

  • Username (required)
  • Password — stored as a one-way hash; we cannot read your password
  • Email address (optional — used only for password recovery)

Platform identifiers

  • Discord user ID — if you link your Discord account
  • WhatsApp phone number — if you use the WhatsApp integration

Game data

  • Character names, party names, and party gold balance
  • Item purchase and sale history (transaction ledger)
  • D&D Beyond character sheet data — only if you choose to link your sheet
  • Personal notes you choose to save
  • Shopping basket contents

Conversation data

  • Messages you send to the shopkeeper, logged to maintain conversation context
  • Session state (e.g. pending confirmations, active intent)

Bug reports and feedback

  • Text you submit via the /report or /feedback commands

Technical data

  • An anonymous web session identifier stored locally on your device (no cookies sent to third parties)

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate you and maintain your account
  • To operate the shopkeeper service — tracking gold, inventory, and game state
  • To send password reset codes via Discord or WhatsApp when requested
  • To investigate bug reports and improve the service
  • To maintain the conversation context that makes the shopkeeper feel coherent

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose.

4. How We Share Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, with the following narrow exceptions:

  • Discord — messages are delivered via the Discord API when you use the bot. Discord’s own privacy policy applies to their platform.
  • Twilio / WhatsApp — messages are delivered via Twilio’s API when you use the WhatsApp integration. Twilio’s and Meta’s privacy policies apply.
  • D&D Beyond — if you choose to link your character sheet, we fetch character data from D&D Beyond on your request.

All other data remains on our own servers and is not disclosed to any other party.

5. Data Retention

Your account and game data are retained for as long as your account is active. Conversation logs are kept to maintain continuity of service. If you delete your account (see below), all personal data associated with it is permanently removed.

6. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your account and all associated data
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we process your data
  • Portability — request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Object — object to processing in certain circumstances

To exercise any of these rights, email us at rpgshopkeeper@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

7. Children

RPG Shop is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Security

Passwords are stored using one-way hashing. Session identifiers are randomly generated UUIDs. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, though no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the service after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
